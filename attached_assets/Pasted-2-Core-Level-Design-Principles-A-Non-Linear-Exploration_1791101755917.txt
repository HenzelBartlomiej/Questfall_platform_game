2. Core Level Design Principles

A. Non-Linear Exploration & Branching Paths (The Turrican Blueprint)

Amiga platformers rarely used simple left-to-right corridors. Design levels with verticality, wide-open exploration, and multiple routes:

Primary Path: Clear and reachable for casual play, but slightly more hazardous.

Alternative Paths: High-risk, high-reward upper platforms requiring precise jumping or power-ups.

Sub-Paths / Underground / Secret Caverns: Broad exploratory areas housing extra collectibles, weapon upgrades, keycards, or secret exits.

B. Visual Aesthetics & Atmosphere (AGA Style)

Copper Gradients: Use smooth, multi-color gradient backdrops to create atmospheric skies or deep cave lighting.

Expressive Character Animation: Mirror games like Aladdin by incorporating squishy, fluid animations for landing, swinging, throwing, and idle states to give the character life.

Distinct Biomes: Standard Amiga structure usually follows 4–5 distinct worlds (e.g., Agrabah Streets, Enchanted Forest, Mechanical Factory, Ancient Ruins, Ice Mountain).

Parallax Layers: Incorporate at least 3 layers of foreground/background depth to give levels scale.

C. Secret Density & Rewards

Amiga players loved hunting for secrets. Incorporate:

Fake Walls/Ceilings: Visually solid tiles that dissolve or let the player pass through into hidden rooms.

Invisible Blocks & Platforms: Secret ledges revealed only when shot or stepped on (a staple of Turrican).

Warp Zones & Bonus Stages: Hidden portals or genie lamps that skip sections or lead to mini-game bonus rooms.

Score & Collectible Synergy: Collecting 100% of items, gems, or specific rare tokens should grant extra lives or access to secret final levels.

3. Interactive Objects & Mechanics

To make the environment engaging and dynamic, include a healthy mix of traversal, hazard, puzzle, and combat objects:

Traversal Objects

Springboards, Trampolines & Bouncing Camels: Launch players to high ledges or secret routes.

Ropes, Poles & Clotheslines: Horizontal and vertical climbing structures allowing swinging jumps across gaps (inspired by Aladdin).

Moving Platforms: Horizontal, vertical, and path-following platforms (both permanent and crumbling/timed).

Morph Wheel / Roll Passages: Narrow tunnels passable only in a compact rolling state (inspired by Turrican's Gyro-wheel).

Ziplines & Teleporters: Fast transport across large gaps or between distant sub-areas.

Conveyor Belts: Alter player movement speed and jump momentum.

Hazards & Obstacles

Spike Strips & Acid Pits: Classic immediate damage or instant-kill zones.

Falling Stalactites / Falling Pots: Environmental hazards dropped from above or triggered by proximity.

Turrets & Environmental Shooters: Flame-throwers, guard posts, or dart cannons on timers requiring rhythm-based traversal.

Interactive Switches & Puzzles

Keycards / Keys / Medallions: Colored keys or collectible tokens that unlock matching doors/gates to progress.

Levers & Pressure Plates: Toggle platform movements, drain water/lava levels, or turn off forcefields.

Destructible Blocks & Cracked Walls: Weak walls or floors that require bombs, sword swings, or heavy weapon fire to clear.

Collectibles & Power-Ups

Basic Collectibles: Coins, gems, or fruit scattered along jump arcs to guide player movement.

Ranged Ammunition: Consumable throwables (e.g., apples, throwing daggers, grenades).

Power-Ups:

Weapon Upgrades: Spread shots, lasers, lightning beams, or bouncing projectiles (inspired by Turrican).

Speed Shoes: Temporary movement boost.

Invincibility Star / Shield: Grants temporary immunity or absorbs hits.

Checkpoint Flag/Terminal/Genie Lamp: Save points that light up and play a brief victory chime when touched.
